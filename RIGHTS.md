# Rights and Preservation

## Historical Notices

I preserve the original source, disk images, archives, binaries and artwork with their existing notices. The source credits Micro Projects in 1985; the packaging identifies Gremlin Graphics Software Limited and carries its 1985 copyright notice. I have not established the present ownership of every right, assigned an open-source licence to the game or relicensed the artwork.

I confirmed on 21 October 2026 that the supplied scans and images are my own, made from magazines or original cassettes I own. This records their provenance. It does not transfer the copyright in the material reproduced in them.

---

## Preservation Practice

I keep historical files byte for byte and generate working files under `build/`. The committed analysis, graphics, screenshots, reports and HTML are documented outputs; caches, intermediate objects and scratch work are ignored. `.gitattributes` protects historical exports from Git newline conversion. `.editorconfig` disables automatic newline and whitespace changes in the preserved collections.

The complete commissioning brief is retained unchanged and linked from the README. Its instructions are a modern project record, not evidence about the original game's authorship or behaviour.

The reused handbook dependencies retain their own licences in `scripts/vendor/`: Marked 17.0.5 and Mermaid 10.9.3. Those licences apply to those libraries, not automatically to the game, graphics or historical sources.
