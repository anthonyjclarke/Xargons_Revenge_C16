# Artifact Catalogue

## Provenance

I confirmed on 21 October 2026 that these are my own images and scans, made from magazines or original cassettes I own. I preserve the supplied files unchanged. Their hashes and categories are recorded in the [input catalogue](verification/input-catalogue.json). Where a publication date or artist is not visible, I leave it unknown.

---

## Cassette and Artwork

![Full original cassette inlay](../Original%20Artifacts/Xargon%27s%20Revenge%20Cassette%20Inlay.jpg)

`Xargon's Revenge Cassette Inlay.jpg` shows the complete Xargon's Revenge inlay, including the astronaut and spacecraft cover, spine, description, screenshots, £6.95 price and Gremlin Graphics notice. Its instruction panel advertises C16/Plus4, 96 levels, 16 waves and three lives. It specifies title selections, keyboard controls, scoring and loading. I compare these instructions with the source rather than treating them as proof of the preserved binary's rules.

`Xargons Revenge Image.jpg` is a closer view of the same cover artwork. `xargons_revenge_front.jpg` shows front, spine and rear packaging with gameplay images. These are Xargon's Revenge material; I found no unrelated game image in these separate scans.

## Screenshots and Compilation Leaflet

![Supplied gameplay screenshot](../Original%20Artifacts/xargons_revenge_main.jpg)

`xargons_revenge_main.jpg` shows the player's ship, alien formation, scrolling landscape, score and fuel display. `Xargons_Revenge.png` shows the title. These are supplied reference images, not screenshots generated by this restoration. The machine, palette and capture settings are not documented in the files, so I do not use them as verification of the modern build.

`C16_Classics_II_English.pdf` is a single scanned compilation leaflet. The lower centre panel contains Xargon's Revenge instructions and a 1985 Gremlin Graphics notice. The surrounding panels concern Monkey Magic, Blagger and Timeslip; those panels are compilation context, not evidence about this game's implementation. The PDF has no usable text layer; I inspected a rendered copy.

The restored title and gameplay screenshots in `docs/images/` are fresh VICE evidence and are labelled separately in [Source and Validation](SOURCE_AND_VALIDATION.md).
